Save and recall from your Personal Brain by texting Poke.

## How Poke works with Supermemory

Poke lets you save something to memory and recall it later by messaging Poke over text. No app switch, no dashboard.

## Before you start

- A Supermemory account with Personal Brain access
- Signed in at [app.supermemory.ai](https://app.supermemory.ai)
- The active organization and Space you want Poke to use

## How to connect Poke to Supermemory

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations in Personal Brain">
    Go to Integrations and select Poke.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Poke recipe">
    The connect flow opens the Poke recipe for Supermemory.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the recipe warning">
    The recipe currently shows an unverified recipe warning. Read what the recipe does before you continue.

    <Frame>
      ![Poke unverified recipe warning](/images/personal-brain/poke-unverified-recipe-warning.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the recipe">
    Confirm in Poke to enable the recipe against your Supermemory account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Message Poke to test">
    Text Poke to save a note, then ask Poke to recall it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How the Poke recipe calls Supermemory

Poke calls Supermemory through the recipe. Messages that save something add a memory to your active Space. Messages that ask for something search your memory and reply with the answer.

## What Poke saves and searches

- Save: the message content you send Poke to save
- Search: your memory, scoped to the Space Poke is connected to

## Limitations of the Poke integration

- The Poke recipe currently displays an unverified recipe warning. Review the recipe before enabling it.
- Saves and recalls run against the Space selected during setup. Switching Spaces requires reconfiguring the recipe.
- Rich content like files is best added from the Supermemory app or a Space aware integration.

## How to troubleshoot Poke

- **Nothing was saved.** Check the active organization and Space, and confirm the Poke recipe is enabled.
- **A recall came back empty.** Confirm the memory finished processing in Personal Brain and that it lives in the Space Poke is pointed at.
- **Poke says it is not connected.** Reopen the recipe from Personal Brain Integrations and confirm the connection status.
